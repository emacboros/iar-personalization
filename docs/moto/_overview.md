## The Decision Framework (law -- built across 4 sessions)

1. **Toy criterion:** "a toy that also happens to be my vehicle." Joy is a first-class requirement, not vanity.
2. **Fun-to-ride > fun-to-look.** Aesthetics may not do illegitimate work in the decision (killed the Calibro Bagger).
3. **One bike.** Two-bike strategy dead: "I'll prefer one and the Svart rots."
4. **Decide for the 49 weeks, not the 3.** Ruta 40 touring demoted; most bikes adaptable for travel later.
5. **5-year ownership horizon.** Not a forever bike.
6. **Primary use:** 40km highway commute 3x/week + weekend riding, now expanding into off-road.
7. **Budget:** 20M ARS ceiling (~13.3k USD at 1500 ARS/USD).
8. **Weird-bike recognition test:** "Can I identify my bike from across the parking lot?"
9. **Test protocol:** ride multiple bikes back-to-back before the decisive test ride -- single-bike baseline means no reference for "normal."

## The Calibration Lesson (read before trusting any review)

Graded 8 reviewer claims against his own Svartpilen at 16k km. Pattern: **physics/character claims TRUE, perception/optics claims FALSE for his unit.** Vibration objectively real (generic mirrors unusable) but invisible to him subjectively. Neutral-finding difficulty: real; he'd blamed himself for 16k km. Consequences: owner forums carry the real issue list; press reviews over-report joy; his tolerance profile is his own -- test rides are the only decisive instrument.

## Training State (started 2026-08-31)

**Goal:** enjoy off-road to the fullest on the new bike. Training window = saving window (~4-5 months to bike budget at 3M ARS/month). The bike is also the reason to stay in shape -- long-term, riding as primary hobby off the computer.

**Plan:** 3-phase base/build/specific (16-20 weeks), then transition when bike arrives, then riding-season steady state. Full plan in tasks/moto/training-plan/. Key principles: zone 2 feels insultingly easy (that's correct), isometric strength-endurance is the specific off-road demand (wall sits, dead hangs, farmer carries), never miss twice, deload every 4th week, running ramps slower than motivation wants (100kg ex-lifter, shin splint risk).

**Equipment:** unused bicycle at home (tune-up week one), good running shoes, home gym (dumbbells + machine). Rucking in the sierras = closest thing to standing on pegs.

**Exit tests Phase 1:** 90min continuous zone 2 conversational. Phase 2: 2h zone 2, 2min wall sit, 90s dead hang. Phase 3: 3h sierra ruck 15kg + fine next morning.
