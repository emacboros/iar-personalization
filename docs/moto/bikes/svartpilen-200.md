# Husqvarna Svartpilen 200 -- the current bike

The baseline. First bike, learned alone, ~16k km as of Aug 2026. 26hp (the 18hp figure was a kW/hp units error). It defined his sense of what a motorcycle is -- which is why the calibration exercise mattered and why back-to-back test rides are mandatory before deciding on the next one.

## What it taught him (the honest ledger)

- Low-end dead below 6k; fun lives in the 6-9k band.
- Vibration: real (generic mirrors unusable), invisible to him subjectively. High tolerance proven.
- Neutral-finding: genuinely hard. He blamed himself for 16k km.
- Highway: cannot cruise 130 without redlining. Cannot overtake trucks safely. This is the actual reason a new bike is on the table.
- Off-road: "don't be afraid when pavement ends" capability, no more. Hard rocks expose physical limits.

## Disposition

Stays as garage sentiment when the new bike arrives. Not for sale per his call; the two-bike strategy is dead but the Svartpilen isn't being evicted.
