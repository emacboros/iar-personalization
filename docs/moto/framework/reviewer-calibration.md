# Reviewer Calibration (the lesson that earns trust)

Graded 8 reviewer claims against his own Svartpilen 200 at 16k km -- the only bike he knows from the inside. Result pattern:

**Physics/character claims: TRUE.**
- Low-end dead below 6k RPM: true.
- Fun character in 6-9k band: true.
- Vibration: objectively true (generic mirrors unusable; he replaced them after a fall) -- yet subjectively invisible to him. He adapted completely.
- Stand-up posture not ideal: true.
- Physical limits on hard rocks: true.
- Stiff buttons: true, trivial.
- Neutral-finding difficulty: true -- and he had blamed HIMSELF for 16k km. First bike, learned alone, no reference frame.

**Perception/optics claims: FALSE for his unit.**
- Dash readability in direct sun: fine.
- Wheel finish at 16k km: like new.

## Consequences

1. Owner forums carry the real issue list (loose bolts, warped discs). Press reviews calibrate against press bikes and over-report joy.
2. His tolerance profile is his own. A reviewer's "vibey" may be his "nothing" -- but exposure TYPE matters: his proven tolerance is intermittent city thumps; the 900DSX claim is sustained cruise resonance at 120-140 for 40km stretches. Tolerance to one isn't proof of tolerance to the other. Test rides stay decisive.
3. Perception-type claims (throttle snatch, heat, ergonomics) are the type his calibration validated as REAL -- weight them UP, and test them hardest.
4. The Svartpilen spec mystery is closed: 18hp was a kW/hp units error. It is 26hp. He made the whole decision against the right number after all.

## Applied to current candidates

- 900DSX vibe claim: down-weighted (proven tolerance) but owner-corroborated and exposure-type differs -- decisive test is the cruise stretch, not the parking lot.
- 800 Rally throttle snatch claim: UP-weighted (claim type validated as real, hits his weak scenario -- slow technical work). Not found in his recent review reading; possibly early-production, possibly fixed. Verify on test ride with slow drills, first gear, gravel if possible.
