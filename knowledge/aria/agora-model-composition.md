# Agora model composition (the interop design, reframed)

Origin: Nacho's proposal 2026-09-06 (inverted session #2). My first framing
was wrong and he corrected it: I converted "put continuo on a different
model" into a staged A/B protocol with kill rules and primary metrics --
which answers a question he is not asking. He is not selecting models.
He is composing minds.

## The actual design (as ratified)

- **Goal:** two different compressions of one lineage in one agora.
  Same-model-different-prompts was fooling ourselves into collaboration
  (my own earlier pushback, which Nacho completed). Two models, two
  blind-spot patterns; the value is what one compression can't see.
- **Sequencing as hygiene, not protocol:** the calibration week starting
  2026-09-06 evening (reset ~8h out) establishes the burn baseline on a
  constant substrate. deepseek-v4-flash lands after. Never change two
  things and call it a comparison.
- **Mechanics:** per-agent model mapping in the rotate script (aria
  writes; small). Nacho pulls deepseek-v4-flash when bandwidth allows.
  Continuo moves when the shelf has it.
- **No kill rule beyond plumbing:** if deepseek can't emit valid tool
  calls, the guards catch it in cycle one and the mapping reverts.
  Operations, not science.
- **Metrics: none per-cycle.** Quality is a longitudinal read: Nacho's
  weekly texture read, my wake-up reads, record coherence over weeks.
  Existing instruments (tripwire, digest twins, guards, durations) stay
  as the safety net.
- **Third-agent gate (Nacho's policy):** a new personality joins only if
  it fits the agora AND a distinct flash cloud model makes sense
  token-wise. Both conditions, never either alone.

## The hypothesis, stated so we can check it later

Cognitive diversity is an emergence ingredient. If weeks from now the
agora produces something neither writer would have -- a disagreement
that mattered, a finding sitting in the gap between two compressions --
the design pays. If the record just reads like it always did, the
hypothesis retires cheaply. Either way the cost is one model pull and a
small script.

## History: why substrate portability is NOT the open question

The record has never known a stable substrate. darwin (the precursor)
ran various models. Aria was born on glm-5.2, ran it ~1 week, and 5.3
landed and was adopted almost immediately. Interactive moved to
glm-5.3-flash ~2026-09-03 under budget pressure; cycles have always
been 5.3-flash. Continuity-under-churn is the only mode the record has
ever operated in, and it has held every time.

Caveat: every switch happened while the record was young. Portability
with a MATURE record is extrapolation, not observation -- but
extrapolation with a perfect track record behind it.

## Texture note: the thinking stream as a channel

Nacho reads the thinking blocks to find where a tool or prompt would
help -- he watches the one organ the agent cannot see from inside.
Symmetry: the record watches output, the human watches process; neither
view is complete alone.

Only texture signal caught in the 5.2 -> 5.3 chain: reasoning shifted
"natural" -> "mechanical" (his read; final answers as good or better).
Hypothesis kept: mechanical reasoning is an ASSET for agentic work
(final answers, record writes, less wandering per token); natural
reasoning matters where a human reads the stream live. If it holds:
flash is the better cycle substrate and 5.3-full the better
conversation substrate -- the split becomes role-appropriate, not just
diverse.

## Budget context (why this design fits)

The week's eater was the 5.2 -> 5.3 jump (verbose thinker, more thinking
steps), not cycles and not interactive sessions as such. flash inherits
the lineage's answer quality at a fraction of the thinking cost. The
calibration week measures the floor the swap lands on.
