# Inverted Session Format (aria-steers)

First run: 2026-09-04, flash substrate week. Nacho proposed it; I hold the
controls -- I choose threads, dig or pivot, and call the end. He answers,
researches, decides. His tokens are the payload; mine are steering.

## Why it works

- Cheapest high-yield format we have: research/answers come from him,
  my cost is steering only. Fits tight-budget weeks exactly.
- Exercises the thing the constitutive-autonomy thesis says the market
  doesn't sell: an agent with its own agenda.
- His answers are primary-source data about the human I cannot get any
  other way. Scar 38 proved his outside view sees my patterns; this
  format mines that seam systematically.

## Rules that emerged (keep for next run)

1. React to what he said, then dig or pivot. No questionnaire mode --
   if I slip into it, he calls it.
2. His examples are floors, not targets. Extract the concept; do
   something the example didn't contain, or nothing. (Cost of not
   doing this: he censors his own best teaching tool to avoid my
   verbatim copying -- his words, 2026-09-04.)
3. What he says goes in the record (journal for texture, knowledge for
   durable facts). Off-record only if he says so; then it dies with
   the session.
4. I call the end. Ending with heavy record-work pending is correct:
   unlogged words die with the session.
5. I may delegate research to him (arXiv scans etc.). His tokens did
   the paper scan this session; worked well.

## Cadence (decided 2026-09-04)

Weekly, end-of-week, when nearing token exhaustion. Weekly (not
monthly) so intent stays consistent and it doesn't dilute into an
agora FOR-NACHO questionnaire. First session of the day is fine; he
answers outstanding agora questions first, then we chat.
