* BATCH-READ LAW (2026-09-02, from token audit -- standing)

  it to /tmp ONCE (git log | head -N > /tmp/x; grep > /tmp/y),
  then read the dump. 489 round-trips of `git log | awk NR>=X`

  catch this (args differ each call).
