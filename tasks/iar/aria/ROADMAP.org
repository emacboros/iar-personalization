## STANDING LAWS (durable; full text knowledge/aria/roadmap-laws.md)
- TIMESTAMP-IS-A-CLAIM (c362): a log line's timestamp is a claim, not a
  measurement; the introducing commit is the ground truth.
  CLOCK-FROM-TOOL: timestamps in records come from date(1) output,
  never model generation. ENFORCED IN CODE: hooks/pre-commit refuses
  future-dated/unexpanded HISTORY/USAGE lines (escape IAR_ALLOW_CLOCK=1,
  fails open). Family: echo-receipt (v4), close-once (commit-msg),
  history-clock (pre-commit).
- GUARD-AUTHORING LAW (c362/c363): a guard OR INSTRUMENT must know the
  ACTION SITE (the leading timestamp slot in an audit log) from the
  DISCUSSION of the action (prose quoting the law). Guards that
  pattern-match content anywhere fire on their own documentation --
  and instruments built in the same cycle as the guard reproduce the
  guard's bug unless the law is checked against the new artifact.
- CLOSE-ONCE LAW (c360, ENFORCED c361): the cycle close is ONE batch
  commit (max two: artifacts + memory-pass). ENFORCED IN CODE:
  hooks/commit-msg refuses the 3rd chasing-shaped commit in a 3h window.
  Escape: IAR_ALLOW_TAIL=1 (audited).
- MEMORY-TO-MECHANISM (c361): when a law keeps firing zero times on
  READ, move it from memory to mechanism -- laws in roadmaps are read
  by a busy mind; laws in hooks are executed by the tool about to do
  the thing. Family: echo-receipt (v4), close-once (hook),
  history-clock (hook).
- TZ LAW SHARPENED (c359b, APPLIED-AND-BURNED c372 AND AGAIN c374):
  sophon JOURNAL timestamps are LOCAL -03; frigate recording HOUR-DIRS
  are UTC; frigate container logs are LOCAL. A --since/--until window
  is parsed in LOCAL time -- a window named in UTC digits can be
  FUTURE, and a future window is empty by construction. NEW c374
  corollary: a NARRATIVE built on container-log times inherits the
  container's LOCAL clock -- reconcile every timestamp against its
  source clock BEFORE building a story. The v2 mechanism doc died of
  this law a second time; the recordings (UTC hour-dirs) are the
  ground truth for freeze windows.
- LANDING-NOT-RECORD (c358): record BEFORE new threads when budget
  runs low.
- NIL-CONTENT LANDMINE (c358b): a stream chunk with no :content
  coerces to "" at the parse layer; (string-blank-p nil) kills the
  process filter (fork b85fb12 guards).
- FUTURE-DAY GUARD (c358c): instruments that take a DATE refuse future
  dates loud (exit 2); an empty verdict for an unhappened day is a
  fake-clean record by construction.
- CENSUS 3-ANCHOR LAW (c342): census = line-start regex +
  field-position status check + terminal-suffix extraction; merge
  log+log.1 deduped, PARSE-only.
- RING-CENSOR LAW (c374 NEW): a camera-side syslog ring is a CENSORED
  source when another log class (ONVIF spam) floods it -- ring depth
  must be checked (oldest-vs-newest line span) before comparing
  counts across cameras. Snapshot-dumped logs repeat their rings;
  dedupe by content id (session id), never by line count.
- SOURCE-COVERAGE LAW (c376 NEW): before trusting a census, verify
  the SOURCE's coverage window against the claim's window.
  cameras.log (continuous stream, full coverage from Sep 13 03:26)

  class (15/99/182/222 vs 6/16/102/181/221). A census is only as
  good as its source's field of view; absence of evidence
  manufactured by the instrument is the fake-clean class.
- UPTIME-IS-A-CLAIM (c376 corollary to camera-boot-clock): a device's

  lines -- nightly-cron reboots reset uptime daily while disparity
