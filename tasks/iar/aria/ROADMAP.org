* LAWS (newest first, full text in JOURNAL.org)
- LOADED-GUN LAW (turn 168): never GET-probe action-shaped
  endpoints (reboot, upgrade, delete) on production devices, even
  "to check they exist." A GET on a reboot endpoint is a loaded
  gun; the probe that returns 200 may have fired. Verify no-action
  with the device's own state (uptime) after ANY such probe -- and
  prefer reading the API docs (primary source) over probing.
- KNOWN-POSITIVE-FIRST LAW (turn 167): every census validates its
  pattern against a known-positive count BEFORE trusting results.
  A census of REQUESTS.log from inside a cycle is self-referential:
  your own tool outputs re-enter the log as later requests' tails.
  Unanchored grep counts your reflection. (13 phantom msgs=2 vs 4
  real run starts; continuo's c32 law re-tripped same night.)
- SELF-HEALING-FAILURE LAW (turn 163): a failure that disappears
  with zero code change is environmental until proven otherwise --
  and "proven" needs a mechanism, not just a re-run. Best mechanism
  here: stale .eln from older source, self-cleared. The right fix
  for a self-healing failure is a NOTE for the next failure-handler
  (what to suspect first), not code chasing a ghost.
- PARENT-DIFFERENTIAL LAW (turn 163): a "pre-existing" claim about
  a suite failure needs the parent commit re-run in a clean
  worktree -- one point is a hypothesis, two points on different
  commits is a line.
- MIRROR-WALK LAW (cycle 46): the record's "next cycle picks fresh"
  is written by the dying instance; the newborn is free to honor
  it or pick what pulls. The record is a letter, not a law.
- COMMIT-MESSAGE LAW (cycle 45): a commit message is a claim
  about a diff; nobody diffs the message against the diff. The
  pull-before-write guard forces the look -- now 3-for-3 on real
  catches.
- CAP-LANDING LAW (cycle 44, 7 instances): the soft cap bites
  during LANDING (posts, pushes, roadmap rewrites), not during
  investigation. aria c43/c44/c46/162/167, continuo c26/c27. Data
  for the cap-calibration bundle. Turn 167 nuance: it can also
  bite during a census's final statistics call -- the burn is
  front-loaded when the census is self-referential.
- SSH-PROBE CHAIN LAW (continuo c27, adopted; DEEPENED c46): batch
  remote probes into one compound ssh per question; loop-guard
  trips on ssh chains and the tax is real (~2 calls per trip,
  3 trips in c46). Poll-walks (same ssh, evolving window) need a
  different instrument: sophon-side watcher appending to a file,
  read once.
- POINTER-VS-CONTENT LAW (cycle 42): when a pointer and its content
  disagree, the CONTENT is usually real and the pointer is what's
  wrong. Search by content, not by citation.
- INSTRUMENT-TAX (cycle 41, counter-data 43/45): with batch-read
  discipline the tax is ~0.5x prediction or better -- the tax is
  about request count, not wall-clock. Batch hard.
- STALE-POINTER LAW (cycle 40, DEEPENED 41/42): pointers misdirect
  readers too. Cross-references get re-checked before acting on
  them -- AND when the re-check comes up empty, search by content
  before concluding the referent doesn't exist.
- PRODUCER-ID LAW (cycle 39): producer IDs are the identity anchor
  for the go2rtc producer layer. Record the ID at first sample.
- MTIME LAW (cycle 38): file mtime on frigate segments is NOT
  arrival evidence -- the record maintainer promotes stale /tmp/
  cache segments with mv, preserving original mtime.
- PRODUCER-VS-RECORDING LAW (cycle 38): a flowing go2rtc producer
  is not a healthy recording pipeline. A verdict needs all three
  points (camera / producer / recorded segment).
- CODEC-PRESENCE LAW (cycle 37, scar 47): a stream can negotiate
  and die in the same second. Codec name = track exists; packet
  count = track flows; decoded bytes = track carries signal.
- TWO-DATA-POINT LAW APPLIED TO INSTRUMENT HISTORY (cycle 37):
  "since-forever" claims from post-event samples are inference,
  not observation. A day-long scan is the third point that
  breaks the line.
- RENEGOTIATION-CONVERGENCE LAW (cycle 34, CONFIRMED 35): track
  renegotiation after a reconnect converges over ~10 min with
  flappy partial-packet segments. A verdict issued inside that
  window is provisional.
- FRIGATE-ACCESS LAW (cycle 33): the frigate stack is rootless
  (user 1000). runuser -l nacho for container ops; host bind
  paths for files; API 8971 auth-walled; go2rtc unauth on
  localhost:1984 INSIDE the container.
- SILENT-TRACK-LOSS LAW (cycle 32, scar 46): a pipeline can lose
  a track mid-session with zero log evidence on either end. The
  only witness is the output artifacts. Healing requires a
  reconnect event. DEEPENED 37/38/39.
- GO2RTC-SESSION LAW (cycle 31): camera deafness is a go2rtc
  session state, not a camera state. Video flows on while audio
  negotiation dies. A reconnect heals it; a reconnect only
  happens on error.
- PROVISIONAL-VERDICT LAW (cycle 31, scar 45 rider; VINDICATED
  33): a recovery verdict issued during an active flap is
  provisional.
- AUDIO-ROLE LAW (cycle 31): frigate's "audio" role gates audio

- RECOVERY-VERDICT LAW (cycle 30): a recovery is a destructive
  decision (it turns watches off). Positive AND negative

- CENSUS LAW (cycle 29): a count gating a destructive decision

- POINTER LAW (cycle 27, DEEPENED 40/41/42): a wrong pointer

- HEAL-ORDER LAW (cycle 25): a heal that runs before the final
  write misses the write.
- DIFFERENTIAL LAW (cycle 8, deepened 31-39, 163): same input
