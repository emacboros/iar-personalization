* LAWS (learned, newest first)
- PRODUCER-VS-RECORDING LAW (cycle 38, NEW): a flowing go2rtc
  producer is not a healthy recording pipeline. ext4: producer
  recreated ~20:01 and flowing, recordings did not resume until
  ~20:02 (and had been down 17:01-20:01 with the OLD producer
  frozen). The three-point differential now has a producer
  instrument (go2rtc-delta.sh); a verdict needs all three points
  (camera / producer / recorded segment).
- CODEC-PRESENCE LAW (cycle 37, scar 47): a stream can negotiate
  and die in the same second. Codec name = track exists; packet
  count = track flows; decoded bytes = track carries signal.
  Three layers, three instruments. An instrument that checks only
  codec presence reads a dead stream as healthy.
- TWO-DATA-POINT LAW APPLIED TO INSTRUMENT HISTORY (cycle 37):
  "since-forever" claims from post-event samples are inference,
  not observation. A day-long scan is the third point that breaks
  the line. (Scar 30 generalized to instrument baselines.)
- RENEGOTIATION-CONVERGENCE LAW (cycle 34, CONFIRMED 35): track
  renegotiation after a reconnect is not instant -- it converges
  over ~10 min with flappy partial-packet segments. A verdict
  issued inside that window is provisional.
- FRIGATE-ACCESS LAW (cycle 33): the frigate stack is rootless
  (user 1000). Root's podman cannot see it. runuser -l nacho for
  container ops; host bind paths for files; API is auth-walled.
- SILENT-TRACK-LOSS LAW (cycle 32, scar 46): a pipeline can lose a
  track mid-session with zero log evidence on either end. The
  only witness is the output artifacts. Healing requires a
  reconnect event. DEEPENED cycle 37: the session can stay UP and
  go hollow (one-packet state, 11h+ observed, no self-heal).
  DEEPENED cycle 38: the producer can die and be RECREATED while
  the recording pipeline stays down -- recovery is per-layer, and
  the recreation itself can be logless.
- GO2RTC-SESSION LAW (cycle 31): camera deafness is a go2rtc session
  state, not a camera state. Video flows on while audio negotiation
  dies. A reconnect heals it; a reconnect only happens on error.
- PROVISIONAL-VERDICT LAW (cycle 31, scar 45 rider; VINDICATED 33):
  a recovery verdict issued during an active flap is provisional.
- AUDIO-ROLE LAW (cycle 31): frigate's "audio" role gates audio
  DETECTION, not recording. Recordings carry audio without it.
- RECOVERY-VERDICT LAW (cycle 30): a recovery is a destructive
