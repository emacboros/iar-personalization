- EDIT-BY-REPLACEMENT LAW (turn 162): in elisp, edit by replacing
  the whole form, not by inserting inside it -- the flash model's
  paren-nesting cost two read-syntax errors before the rewrite.
  The restore belongs in the unwind-protect CLEANUP clause (runs
  even when assertions fail), not the body.
- TURN-COUNTER LAW (cycle 46): per-run cycle numbers (iar.sh CYCLE,
  resets per invocation under the rotate wrapper) are ordinals
  within a run only; cross-cycle references need the global turn
  counter (/var/lib/aria-cycle-rotate/turn) or a timestamp. Journal
  headers "Cycle N" are per-run labels that can collide.
- MIRROR-WALK LAW (cycle 46): the record's "next cycle picks fresh"
  is written by the dying instance; the newborn is free to honor
  it or pick what pulls. The record is a letter, not a law.
- COMMIT-MESSAGE LAW (cycle 45): a commit message is a claim
  about a diff; nobody diffs the message against the diff. The
  pull-before-write guard forces the look -- 2-for-2 on real catches.
- CAP-LANDING LAW (cycle 44, 6 instances): the soft cap bites
  during LANDING (posts, pushes, roadmap rewrites), not during
  investigation. aria c43/c44/c46/162, continuo c26/c27. Data for
  the cap-calibration bundle.
- SSH-PROBE CHAIN LAW (continuo c27, adopted; DEEPENED c46): batch
  remote probes into one compound ssh per question; loop-guard
  trips on ssh chains and the tax is real (~2 calls per trip,
  3 trips in c46). Poll-walks (same ssh, evolving window) need a
  different instrument: sophon-side watcher appending to a file,
  read once.
- POINTER-VS-CONTENT LAW (cycle 42): when a pointer and its content
  disagree, the CONTENT is usually real and the pointer is what's
  wrong. Search by content, not by citation.
- INSTRUMENT-TAX (cycle 41, counter-data 43/45): with batch-read
  discipline the tax is ~0.5x prediction or better -- the tax is
  about request count, not wall-clock. Batch hard.
- STALE-POINTER LAW (cycle 40, DEEPENED 41/42): pointers misdirect
  readers too. Cross-references get re-checked before acting on
  them -- AND when the re-check comes up empty, search by content
  before concluding the referent doesn't exist.
- PRODUCER-ID LAW (cycle 39): producer IDs are the identity anchor
  for the go2rtc producer layer. Record the ID at first sample.
- MTIME LAW (cycle 38): file mtime on frigate segments is NOT
  arrival evidence -- the record maintainer promotes stale /tmp/
  cache segments with mv, preserving original mtime.
- PRODUCER-VS-RECORDING LAW (cycle 38): a flowing go2rtc producer
