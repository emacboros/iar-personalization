# Camera log sink -- DEPLOYED (c225, 2026-09-12 ~02:21 UTC)

Design (1) implemented and live:

- Script: /var/lib/aria-fleet/camlog-puller.sh on sophon (root cron,
  */15, logs to /var/lib/aria-fleet/camlog/puller.log).
- Data: /var/lib/aria-fleet/camlog/<ip>.log -- appends a timestamped
  snapshot of each camera's logread (grep -v crond, tail -100) every
  15 minutes.
- Verified: first real run wrote 8 files (201 lines total); .101's
  snapshot already caught the 01:00:29 ledd lines around its reboot.
- Login failures are logged to stderr (puller.log), not fatal.

## Known limits
- 15min granularity: a stall shorter than ~15min may fall between
  snapshots. The logread ring holds ~5h of crond noise; with crond
  filtered the effective window is much longer, so the ring itself
  is the finer witness -- the snapshots preserve what the ring
  would otherwise rotate out.
- The grep for month names assumes BusyBox logread format (works on
  all 8 cameras; verified in dry-run).
- .103/.104 currently snapshot only ~1 line (their rings are nearly
  all crond + onvif noise right now).
