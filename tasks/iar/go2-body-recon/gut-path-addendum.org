# GUT-PATH addendum (2026-09-03, same session -- supersedes priorities)

Nacho pivoted the plan: buy the Go2 AIR for the HW alone
(servos, body, IMU, battery) and GUT the control board,
replacing it with his own FPGA-based controller. He is
electronically/software focused, NOT mechanical -- the Go2
chassis is the bought mechanical miracle. "Put Linux in it"
now means: FPGA fabric = servo loops (reflexes), SoC/SBC =
gait policy (Linux), agora-mind = intent over network.

This KILLS the firmware-unlock question (item 1 in the
original brief drops to footnote priority -- the gut path
does not care about Unitree's software politics).

NEW load-bearing questions, in order:

1. **Go2 motor protocol RE state.** What do the Go2 (Air)
   joint motors actually speak (CAN? variant? encrypted?).
   Community reverse-engineering status as of Sep 2026:
   Go1 motor CAN frames were documented; Go2 status UNKNOWN
   to us. Any project that has driven Go2 motors from an
   EXTERNAL controller? This single answer is the go/no-go
   for the whole gut path.
2. **BMS behavior.** Does the Go2 battery refuse to discharge
   without its stock master board alive? Any RE of the BMS
   protocol? (Fallback if locked: stock board stays powered
   as a "BMS interpreter" while gutted of control.)
3. **Calibration data.** Where do per-joint calibration and
   IMU calibration live (motion board flash? main board?
   motor boards?). Anything that must be DUMPED BEFORE
   gutting. LAW: image every flash chip before desolder.
4. **Air vs Pro actuator parity.** Same motors/battery/body?
   If identical actuators, Air at ~$1600 is the parts
   bargain and Pro adds nothing for a gut build. If Pro has
   better motors, that changes the math.
5. **Open-source locomotion policies for Go2.** The RL
   sim-to-real ecosystem (Isaac Lab / MuJoCo-trained policies
   outputting joint targets at ~50Hz) -- what exists, what
   hardware it assumes, whether the policy tier can run on a
   modest SoC/SBC. Go2 is THE research platform; inventory
   the best-maintained options.
6. **Prior gut attempts.** Anyone who bypassed the Unitree
   motion controller entirely (not dev-mode -- full bypass)?
   Search: "Go2 motor driver external", "Go2 CAN protocol",
   FPGA quadruped control, MITM quadruped bus.

Original brief items that still matter: AR import path
