* Phase 1 -- Base (weeks 1-6)

Aerobic foundation + isometric habit. Weekly template in docs/moto/training/weekly-templates.md.

**Week 1 special:** bicycle tune-up (tires, brakes, chain, seat height) before trusting it at 90min. Running starts walk-run 4min/1min -- at 100kg ex-lifter, shin splints are the 3-week trap.

**Weekly structure:** Mon strength A / Tue zone 2 bike 45-60 / Wed walk-run / Thu strength B / Fri zone 2 bike 60-75 / Sat long easy 75-90 / Sun rest + mobility. Wall sits 3x45-60s and dead hangs daily-ish.

**Zone 2 discipline:** conversational pace or slower. It will feel insultingly easy. That's correct. If he can't speak full sentences, it's junk intensity.

**Deload:** every 4th week, volume -30%.

**Exit test:** 90min continuous zone 2, conversational throughout. Log the attempt date and result in LOGS.
