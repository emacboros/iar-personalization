* Phase 2 -- Build (weeks 7-12)

Intervals enter (week 5-6 earliest, not before -- the motivated-person error is starting hard and dying by week 3). Rucking becomes a real session: sierras, 10-15kg. Wall sits to 2-3min, dead hangs to 90s+ then towel over bar.

**Weekly structure:** Mon strength A / Tue zone 2 bike 75-90 / Wed intervals (hill repeats 5-6x3min or run 6x2min) / Thu strength B + split-squat isometric holds / Fri zone 2 run 40-50 continuous / Sat long day 2h+ / Sun easy or rest.

**Exit tests:** 2h continuous zone 2; wall sit 2min; dead hang 90s.
