* Phase 3 -- Specific (weeks 13-18, until the bike exists)

Back-to-back days: long Saturday 2-3h, medium Sunday 60-90min deliberately. Day-after capacity is what multi-day riding demands. Tempo session 30-40min sustained comfortably-hard simulates a long technical stretch.

**Weekly structure:** Mon strength A / Tue zone 2 bike 90 / Wed intervals 6-8x3min or tempo / Thu strength B / Fri zone 2 run 45-60 / Sat long 2-3h / Sun medium 60-90.

**Exit test (the real one):** 3h sierra ruck with 15kg, feel fine the next morning, and go do it again Sunday.

**Skills in parallel:** off-road intro course booked; parking-lot drills weekly (tight circles, figure-8s, clutch work -- doubles as throttle-snatch verification for the 800). Boots purchased before trails.
