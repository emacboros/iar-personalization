* Phase 4 -- Transition (bike arrives, first 4-6 weeks)

Riding is now the primary training but it's novel fatigue. Rules:

- First rides replace 1-2 zone 2 sessions, not all
- Ride length +10-15%/week, no jumps
- Arm pump ends first trail days hours before legs do -- normal, fades 4-6 weeks
- Parking-lot drills weekly
- Strength drops to 2x short maintenance
- Intro off-road course before first real trail day
